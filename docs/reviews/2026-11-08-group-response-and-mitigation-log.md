## Executive Assessment of the Critique
We evaluated all 19 findings (`F1`–`F19`) against our local source files ([MyCogTechSpec.md](../../MyCogTechSpec.md), [earlycognitivescreeningnovel-approach-amid-current.pdf](../../earlycognitivescreeningnovel-approach-amid-current.pdf)), the cited external clinical consensus papers (Crutch et al., 2017; McKeith et al., 2017; Gorno-Tempini et al., 2011; Mamiya et al., 2016; Borchers et al., 2013; Hammers et al., 2020), the `google/xrblocks` (`v0.24.0`) library, the Gemini API Terms of Service (effective March 23, 2026), and FDA's Clinical Decision Support guidance (January 29, 2026).

**Conclusion:** The critique is **rigorous, mathematically accurate, and clinically sound**. Every statistical calculation in the review (`F1a` Wilson intervals, `F4` RCI reliability thresholds and regression-to-the-mean expectations, `F10` within-condition trimming / CoMAD motor-subtraction bias / RSI instability, `F11` $0.488$ family-wise false-trigger rate) was independently verified by us and confirmed correct. We have **Accepted** 18 findings in full and **Accepted in part** 1 minor framing judgment (`F16/F2` prototype-vs-clinical dual mode), and we have applied all mitigations across the repository.
